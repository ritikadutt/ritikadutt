## A little about me

I'm Ritika. I started with the web because I loved the immediacy of it: write a few lines, refresh the page, and there it is. Machine learning pulled me in for almost the opposite reason—sometimes the answer is messy, probabilistic, and hidden in the data.

These days, I enjoy working where the two meet. That might mean building an interface around a model, turning a notebook into an API, figuring out why a pipeline behaves perfectly on my laptop and nowhere else, or removing a feature that seemed clever but made the product harder to use.

I care about readable code, honest metrics, accessible interfaces, and software that earns its complexity. I'm especially interested in NLP, recommendation systems, computer vision, LLM applications, and the less glamorous engineering needed to make them reliable.

Outside of shipping things, you'll usually find me reading, taking notes on an idea I'll probably revisit six months later, or opening one more browser tab than my laptop would prefer.
